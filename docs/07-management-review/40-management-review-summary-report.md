## 3. ISMS performance overview

**Ratings**

- **Green:** target met, or on plan.
- **Amber:** below target but within the agreed recovery plan, or less than 10% off target.
- **Red:** off target with no recovery plan, or 10% or more off target, or a major nonconformity open.

Every rating comes from a figure in the minutes.
