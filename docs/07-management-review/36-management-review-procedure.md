## 1. Purpose

Top management reviews the ISMS at planned intervals to decide whether it remains:

| Test | Question top management answers |
| --- | --- |
| **Suitable** | Does the ISMS still fit Northstar's purpose, context and risks? |
| **Adequate** | Is it sufficient to meet ISO/IEC 27001, legal, contractual and customer requirements? |
| **Effective** | Is it achieving its intended outcomes: objectives met, risks reduced, incidents handled? |

Management review is a formal **evaluation that ends in decisions,** not a meeting to discuss policies. Its minutes are the main evidence that top management leads the ISMS (clause 5.1).

## 2. Scope

The whole ISMS within the scope in NSD-ISMS-004: the SaaS platform, Azure (UK and EU regions), Microsoft 365, corporate IT, all personnel in the UK and EU, and relevant third-party dependencies.

## 3. Frequency

| Review | When | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Full management review** | **At least annually.** The first is in **September 2027** (charter month 10), after internal audit cycle 1 and before Stage 1 | Every input in section 5; formal conclusion on suitability, adequacy and effectiveness |
| **Interim review** | Six months after certification, during the first certification cycle | Progress on actions, risks, objectives and audit findings |
| **Extraordinary review** | Within 20 working days of a trigger | The trigger and its effect on the ISMS |

**Triggers for an extraordinary review:**

- a Severity 1 incident (NSD-ISMS-027);
- a major nonconformity from an internal or external audit;
- a significant change to the business, technology or scope, such as a new product, country or major supplier;
- a material change in legal or contractual requirements, such as the Cyber Security and Resilience Bill becoming law, or a new regulated FS customer;
- a new Very High risk, or a Very High acceptance needing renewal (NSD-ISMS-016).

**The monthly ISMS Steering Group** handles day-to-day monitoring: risks, treatment, actions and measures. Its reports feed the management review, but they **do not replace it.**

## 4. Roles and attendance

| Role | Responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| **Managing Director (chair)** | Chairs the review; makes and approves decisions; allocates resources; approves the minutes. **The review cannot proceed without the MD** |
| ISMS Steering Group members (IT Manager, Head of Engineering, HR Manager, Finance Manager, GRC Analyst) | Attend; present performance in their areas; propose improvements and resource needs |
| Data Protection lead | Presents data protection, breach and legal changes |
| Risk owners (NSD-ISMS-017) | Present their risks and treatment status; propose acceptance decisions |
| Internal auditor | Presents audit results **directly**, not through the GRC Analyst (NSD-ISMS-030 §3) |
| **GRC Analyst (coordinator)** | Prepares the agenda and input pack; checks the data; takes minutes; tracks actions. **Does not decide** |

**Quorum:** the MD, plus the IT Manager, the Head of Engineering and at least one other Steering Group member.

## 5. Required inputs (clause 9.3.2)

Each input comes from an existing ISMS record. The detailed register is NSD-ISMS-037.

| Clause 9.3.2 | Input | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| (a) | Status of actions from previous management reviews | NSD-ISMS-039 |

| (c) | Changes in interested parties' needs and expectations | NSD-ISMS-003 |
| (d)(1) | Nonconformities and corrective actions: trends | NSD-ISMS-034 |
| (d)(2) | Monitoring and measurement results: trends | Policy measures (NSD-ISMS-023 to 029 §12); NSD-ISMS-021 |
| (d)(3) | Audit results | NSD-ISMS-030 to 035; external audits |
| (d)(4) | Fulfilment of information security objectives | Charter Obj 1–10 (NSD-ISMS-001 §2) |
| (e) | Feedback from interested parties | Customer questionnaires and complaints; insurer; certification body |
| (f) | Results of risk assessment and status of the risk treatment plan | NSD-ISMS-014 to 017, 020 |
| (g) | Opportunities for continual improvement | Improvement actions (NSD-ISMS-034 §5); audit OFIs; staff suggestions |

**Additional inputs:**

- adequacy of resources (charter §8.1);
- incidents and lessons learned (NSD-ISMS-027);
- SoA changes (NSD-ISMS-019);
- supplier performance (NSD-ISMS-026);
- legal and regulatory changes (NSD-ISMS-005).

**Input quality**

- Each input shows a **trend** across periods, not a single figure, where data exists.
- Each figure gives its source and data cut-off date.
- The GRC Analyst checks figures against the source records before the pack is issued.

## 6. Required outputs (clause 9.3.3)

The review must produce **recorded decisions**, not just discussion:

1. **A formal conclusion** on whether the ISMS is suitable, adequate and effective, with reasons.
2. **Decisions on continual improvement** opportunities.
3. **Decisions on any changes to the ISMS:** scope, policies, objectives, controls or SoA.
4. **Resource decisions:** people, budget and tools, with amounts.
5. **Risk decisions** within the MD's authority: acceptances, renewals and treatment priorities (NSD-ISMS-016).
6. **Changes to objectives or targets,** if any.
7. **Actions,** each with one accountable owner, a due date and a completion measure.

## 7. Process

| Step | Timing | Who |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Set the date and data cut-off | 6 weeks before | GRC Analyst, with the MD |
| 2. Collect and check inputs from the source records | 4 to 2 weeks before | GRC Analyst; input owners |
| 3. Issue the agenda and input pack (NSD-ISMS-037) | **5 working days before** | GRC Analyst |
| 4. Hold the review; record decisions and actions (NSD-ISMS-038) | Review day | MD (chair); attendees |
| 5. Issue draft minutes; MD approves them | Within **5 working days** | GRC Analyst; MD |
| 6. Enter actions in NSD-ISMS-039; publish the summary report (NSD-ISMS-040) | Within 5 working days | GRC Analyst |
| 7. Tell relevant staff about the decisions that affect them (clause 7.4) | Within 10 working days | Action owners; MD |

## 8. Follow-up

- Every action is tracked in NSD-ISMS-039 and reviewed at each monthly Steering Group meeting.
- **Overdue actions:** reported to the Steering Group; more than 30 days overdue, escalated to the MD.
- **Closure needs evidence.** The GRC Analyst verifies it, or the internal auditor does where the GRC Analyst owns the action.
- The status of all actions is input (a) to the next review.
