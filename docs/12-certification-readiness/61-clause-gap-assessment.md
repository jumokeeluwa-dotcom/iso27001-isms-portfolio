## 1. Purpose

To assess how far Northstar's ISMS meets clauses 4–10 of ISO/IEC 27001:2022, and to identify what must be fixed before a certification audit. Results feed the readiness report (NSD-ISMS-065) and the remediation tracker (NSD-ISMS-068).

## 2. Method

A single scale cannot show a process that is operating but not approved. Understating evidence is as inaccurate as overstating it.

So each clause is assessed on **two separate questions:**

| Dimension | Values |
| --- | --- |
| **Design:** is the process documented and approved? | **Approved** · **Drafted** (exists, not approved) · **None** |
| **Operation:** do records show it working? | **Evidenced** (complete records in the last 12 months) · **Partial** (some records; gaps or inconsistencies) · **None** · **Unknown** (claimed, but no record) |

| Readiness | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| **Ready** | Approved **and** Evidenced |
| **Partly ready** | Drafted or Approved, and Partial or Evidenced |
| **Not ready** | Design None, or Operation None or Unknown |

The same method is used for the Annex A review (NSD-ISMS-062).

## 6. Conclusion

Northstar's ISMS is **substantially designed and partly operating.** It has real operating evidence in clauses 9.1, 9.2 and 10: a measured dashboard, one internal audit, and corrective actions with root cause analysis. **No management review has been recorded.** It is **not ready for Stage 1,** because the documents an auditor checks first (scope, policy, risk criteria, SoA) are unapproved or incomplete, and top management has not yet reviewed the ISMS. It is not ready for Stage 2, because 8 of 9 planned internal audits and several key control tests have no record.
