## 1. Purpose

This policy sets out how everyone at Northstar may use company information, devices, systems and services, so that work is done securely wherever staff are based.

## 2. Scope

It applies to employees, contractors, temporary staff and other authorised users, in the UK office, at home in the UK and in the EU. It covers company devices, Microsoft 365, the SaaS platform, and any personal mobile device used to access company data.

## 3. General responsibilities

Users must:

1. use company information and systems for authorised business purposes. Reasonable, occasional personal use is allowed if it does not affect work, security or the law;
2. handle information according to its classification (NSD-ISMS-008, NSD-ISMS-025);
3. protect their passwords, MFA methods and devices (NSD-ISMS-023);
4. report suspected security incidents **immediately** (NSD-ISMS-027);
5. complete security awareness training at induction and every year;
6. acknowledge this policy at induction and after each revision.

## 4. Prohibited activities

Users must not:

1. access information without a business need and authorisation;
2. share passwords, MFA codes or accounts;
3. install software that is not approved through IT Operations;
4. bypass or disable security controls, including encryption, device management, endpoint protection, web filtering and DLP;
5. send Confidential or Restricted information to personal email, personal cloud storage or unapproved services;
6. disclose company or customer information to anyone not authorised to receive it;
7. use company systems for anything unlawful, harassing or malicious.

## 5. Generative AI tools

1. Only **AI tools approved by IT Operations** may be used for work (T-19.1).
2. **Restricted information must never be entered into any AI tool,** approved or not. This includes customer platform data, HR records, credentials and vulnerability details (NSD-ISMS-008 §4).
3. Confidential information may be entered only into approved enterprise AI tools that have contractual data protection.
4. Users remain responsible for checking AI output before using it.

Unapproved AI sites are blocked, and uploads of Restricted-labelled content are stopped by DLP (T-19.2).

## 6. Email, messaging and internet

1. Treat unexpected attachments, links and requests for information or payment with caution.
2. Report suspected phishing using the **Report** button in Outlook.
3. Requests to change bank details or make urgent payments are always verified by calling a known number, never by replying (NSD-ISMS-015, T-04.1).
4. Use only approved services for sharing files and messages: Microsoft 365 and approved collaboration tools.

## 7. Devices

1. Company laptops are encrypted, managed through device management and protected by endpoint security. Users must not interfere with these.
2. **Restricted information must not be stored locally on any endpoint** (NSD-ISMS-008 §4). It stays in approved Restricted locations.
3. Screens lock automatically after 5 minutes of inactivity. Users lock their screen whenever they step away.
4. **Personal mobile devices** may access company email and Teams only through the approved, managed app with app protection, and only after enrolment.
5. Removable media follows NSD-ISMS-008: company-encrypted only, and **never for Restricted information.**
6. Devices are returned on or before the last working day (NSD-ISMS-023 §9).

## 8. Remote and hybrid working

1. Work only from places where screens and conversations cannot be overseen or overheard.
2. Connect through the approved secure access method on networks you do not control, including public Wi-Fi.
3. Keep devices with you or locked away. Never leave them in a vehicle.
4. Report a lost or stolen device **immediately**, so it can be wiped remotely.
5. **Working from outside the UK or EU** with company data or devices needs prior approval from the IT Manager and the Data Protection lead, because it may involve an international data transfer.
6. Keep a clear desk at home and in the office. Paper with Confidential or Restricted information is locked away and shredded when no longer needed.

## 9. Monitoring

Northstar monitors the use of its systems to protect information and meet legal duties. Examples include sign-in logs, DLP alerts, web filtering and endpoint security.

Monitoring is lawful, proportionate and transparent. It is described in the employee privacy notice, follows UK and EU employment and data protection law (EXT-09), and is supported by a data protection impact assessment. Monitoring data is accessed only for security, legal or HR purposes with authorisation.

## 10. Non-compliance and exceptions

- Breaches are handled as security incidents and may lead to disciplinary action (Annex A 6.4), or contractual action for contractors.
- Exceptions follow the single exceptions process (NSD-ISMS-008 §8).
