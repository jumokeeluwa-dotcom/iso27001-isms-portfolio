## 1. Purpose

Northstar Digital Solutions Ltd protects the confidentiality, integrity and availability of the information entrusted to it by customers, employees and partners.

This policy sets management's direction for information security. It is the framework for establishing, implementing, maintaining and continually improving Northstar's Information Security Management System (ISMS). All topic-specific security policies (section 9) sit beneath it.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to:

- all employees and contractors, in the UK head office, working from home in the UK, and based in the EU;
- senior management and department managers;
- all information and assets inside the ISMS scope (NSD-ISMS-004): the SaaS platform, Microsoft Azure (UK and EU regions), Microsoft 365, corporate IT and company devices;
- suppliers and other third parties that access or process Northstar information or support in-scope services.

## 3. Management commitment

The Managing Director, on behalf of top management, commits Northstar to:

1. **Meet applicable requirements:** legal, regulatory and contractual information security obligations (clause 5.2(c)).
2. **Continually improve** the ISMS (clause 5.2(d)).
3. **Provide resources** for the ISMS, including the people and budget agreed in the charter (NSD-ISMS-001 §8).
4. **Set measurable objectives** and review progress at least annually at management review.
5. **Make risk-based decisions** within the approved risk appetite and acceptance criteria (NSD-ISMS-016).

## 5. Principles

1. **Risk-based:** controls follow from assessed risk (NSD-ISMS-012) and are recorded in the Statement of Applicability (NSD-ISMS-019).
2. **Least privilege and need-to-know:** access is limited to what each role requires (NSD-ISMS-023).
3. **Classified handling:** information is protected according to its classification (NSD-ISMS-008).
4. **Evidence over assertion:** a control counts as effective only when it has been tested (NSD-ISMS-021).
5. **Security by design:** security is built into the platform, changes and supplier choices from the start.

## 7. Legal, regulatory and contractual requirements

Northstar's obligations are recorded in NSD-ISMS-003 and, in detail, in the legal register (NSD-ISMS-005). They include:

- UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018, as amended by the Data (Use and Access) Act 2025;
- EU GDPR, because of EU-based personnel, EU-hosted data and EU customers;
- customer contracts and data processing agreements, including Northstar's duties as a processor;
- DORA-related terms passed down from financial services customers.

**Internal risk acceptance cannot waive an external obligation** (NSD-ISMS-016 §4).

## 8. Incident reporting

Everyone covered by this policy must report a suspected or actual security incident **immediately** through the designated reporting channel. Incidents are assessed and handled under the Incident Management Policy (NSD-ISMS-027). This includes notifying customers as their processor, and supporting regulator notification within 72 hours where required.

## 10. Compliance and exceptions

- Breaches of this policy are handled as security incidents and may lead to disciplinary action or, for suppliers, contractual action.
- **Exceptions** follow one process (NSD-ISMS-008 §8). They are requested to the GRC Analyst, risk-assessed, approved by the asset owner (Confidential) or the MD (Restricted), limited to 12 months and logged.
- Compliance is checked through internal audit and the evidence tests in NSD-ISMS-021.

## 11. Communication and review

- The policy is published on the company intranet and acknowledged by all staff and contractors at induction and after each revision. The target is **95% acknowledgement** (NSD-ISMS-021, control 5.1).
- Once approved, it is available to customers, auditors and other interested parties on request.
- It is reviewed annually at management review, and after significant change.
