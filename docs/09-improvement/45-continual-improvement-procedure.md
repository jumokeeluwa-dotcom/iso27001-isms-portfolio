## 1. Purpose

Northstar continually improves the suitability, adequacy and effectiveness of its ISMS (clause 10.1). This procedure sets out how improvement opportunities are found, triaged, prioritised, approved, implemented and **verified**.

The principle running through it: **do not just fix and move on.** Understand the cause, look for the same weakness elsewhere, act, and check the action worked.

## 2. Improvement or nonconformity?

Every item is triaged first, so it is handled once, in one register:

| Question | If yes | Process and register |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Is a requirement not met? (ISO/IEC 27001, a policy, a contract, the law) | **Nonconformity** | NSD-ISMS-046 procedure; NSD-ISMS-034 register |
| Is a risk treatment action needed? | **Risk treatment** | NSD-ISMS-015 |
| Otherwise: is there a way to do better, reduce risk further, or work more efficiently? | **Improvement** | This procedure; NSD-ISMS-047 register |

If an improvement later turns out to involve a requirement not being met, it moves to NSD-ISMS-046. If a corrective action shows a wider improvement opportunity, that opportunity is raised here, with a cross-reference.

## 4. Process

| Step | Activity | Who |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Raise:** describe the opportunity and its source | Anyone |
| 2 | **Triage** (section 2); record it in NSD-ISMS-047 with an IMP-nn ID | GRC Analyst, within 5 working days |
| 3 | **Assess:** the benefit (which risk or KPI it improves), the effort, and whether it is mandatory | GRC Analyst, with the proposed owner |
| 4 | **Prioritise** using section 5 | GRC Analyst |
| 5 | **Plan the change** (clause 6.3, section 7), where it changes the ISMS | Owner |
| 6 | **Approve** at the level set in section 6; assign an owner, date and **success measure** | Approver |
| 7 | **Implement:** technical changes go through change management (NSD-ISMS-029 §5) | Owner |
| 8 | **Verify:** check that the success measure was achieved, not only that the work was done | GRC Analyst, or the internal auditor where the GRC Analyst owns the item |
| 9 | **Close:** record the result and lessons learned; tell the person who raised it | GRC Analyst |

Items that are not taken forward are recorded as **"Declined"** with the reason. That shows they were considered, not ignored.

## 5. Prioritisation

| Factor | Rating |
| --- | --- |
| **Mandatory?** | Required by law, contract or certification: **yes or no** |
| **Benefit** | **High:** reduces a High or Very High risk, or moves a Red KPI. **Medium:** reduces a Medium risk or moves an Amber KPI. **Low:** efficiency or quality only |
| **Effort** | **Low:** under 5 person-days, no spend. **Medium:** 5–20 person-days, or spend within the owner's budget. **High:** over 20 person-days, or new budget |

| Result | Priority |
| --- | --- |
| Mandatory | **P1:** schedule now |
| High benefit, low or medium effort | **P1:** quick win |
| High benefit, high effort | **P2:** plan, and take to management review for resources |
| Medium benefit | **P2** or **P3**, depending on effort |
| Low benefit, low effort | **P3:** do when capacity allows |
| Low benefit, high effort | **Declined,** with the reason recorded |

## 6. Approval

| Improvement | Approved by |
| --- | --- |
| Within one team; no extra spend; no change to policy or procedure | The owner (risk or control owner) |
| Changes a procedure, affects several teams, or needs up to 20 person-days | ISMS Steering Group |
| Changes a policy, the scope, objectives or the SoA, or needs new budget (illustratively over £5,000) | Managing Director |

## 7. Planning changes to the ISMS (clause 6.3)

When an improvement changes the ISMS (scope, policies, processes, controls, roles or objectives), the owner records:

1. **Purpose** of the change and its potential consequences, including any new risk.
2. **Integrity of the ISMS:** which other documents, controls or registers must change too.
3. **Resources** needed.
4. **Responsibilities:** who does what, and who approves.

The GRC Analyst checks that every affected document is updated and version-controlled. This is the same cross-document check used throughout the portfolio.

## 8. Monitoring and reporting

| Measure | Reported to |
| --- | --- |
| Open improvements by priority; overdue P1 items | Steering Group, monthly |
| Improvements verified effective vs implemented | Steering Group, monthly |
| Improvements raised by staff | Management review (encourages participation) |
| Summary of improvements and their effect on KPIs and risks | Management review, input 9.3.2(g) |

Overdue P1 improvements are escalated to the Steering Group, and to the MD after 30 days. An improvement that did not achieve its success measure is reopened or declined, with the reason recorded.

## 9. Roles

| Role | Responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| Managing Director | Approves significant changes and resources; reviews improvement priorities at management review |
| ISMS Steering Group | Approves cross-team improvements; monitors progress |
| Information Security / GRC Analyst | Maintains NSD-ISMS-047; triages; coordinates; checks ISMS integrity; verifies, except for GRC-owned items |
| Risk and control owners | Raise, plan and deliver improvements; provide evidence |
| All staff | Raise suggestions |

## 10. Records

The register (NSD-ISMS-047), proposals, assessments, approvals, change plans, implementation evidence and verification results are kept for **3 years**. Records showing weaknesses are classified Confidential.
