## Overview

I built a complete, simulated ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Information Security Management System (ISMS) for a fictional UK SaaS company, Northstar Digital Solutions Ltd. The project runs from project charter to mock certification audit: **67 documents across 12 milestones,** each traceable to the others and challenged before acceptance. It complements my practical GRC experience in banking and professional services.

## The case study

A fictional B2B SaaS provider of about 85 people, with a UK head office and EU-based staff, running on Microsoft Azure (UK and EU regions), Microsoft 365 and Entra ID. Its customers include UK and EU financial services firms with DORA contract requirements. Standard: ISO/IEC 27001:2022 including Amendment 1:2024.

## How I worked

- **Evidence over assertion:** missing records are not success, "no events" is never 100%, and nobody verifies their own work.
- **Drafts are challenged, and errors are corrected openly:** every document's change log records what was found and fixed, including two of my own mistakes, which I traced through every affected document.
- **Honest conclusions:** the readiness assessment concludes the ISMS is **not yet ready for Stage 1,** and shows why, with owners and dates for each gap.
